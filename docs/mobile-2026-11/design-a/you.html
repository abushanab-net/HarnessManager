Notification settings

Each event on its own switch.

  • Thresholds are inline links that open a sheet (Limit low below 20%, harvest 30 m before).
  • Requests can break quiet hours; your choice, on by default.
  • A test notification proves the whole path and reports the last delivery.
S21 · sheet

Limit low threshold

Opened from “Below 20% ›” in Notifications.

  • The threshold is a Calibre dial with an amber arc and a knob to drag, plus presets (10, 20, 30, 50%) and the window it watches.
  • It says what would happen right now: claude-main is already at 11%, so one alert would go out.
  • Per-account overrides, and the accounts it skips because they report no limit window.

Earned moments

The delight is kept for things that were earned, and each moment runs once, in about 600 ms. With Reduce Motion they land in their end state. New in the full phase: the tick that lands when a request is answered (S9, S10, S12), the date wheel turning on Home, You and Progress, and the first-sync count on Connected (S5). None of them is tied to approvals or spend.

Harvest

Saturday 18:58, if Dev01 is used first: its week resets with 4% unused. The reserve winds up, a lume tick lands, +40 rises and settles.

Level-up

Crossing 1,500 XP. The reserve winds to full, one sweep of lume ticks, the new name sets in. No confetti, no sound by default, one light haptic.
