Job one and two in four screens: Home answers “where do I keep working?”, and a request is reached from any tab in one tap, answered with an honest countdown, and confirmed with a receipt. Countdowns here run live; the question expires after 41 s, as it would.

Needs you, from every tab

One pill in the large-title bar of every tab: a soft vermilion wash, the count, and the time left on the soonest request. It is the only pulse in the app. In a pushed screen it moves into the compact nav bar (Account detail, Progress). When nothing waits it shrinks to a quiet tray button, so the inbox is always one tap away and the bar never jumps.

S8

Requests inbox

Every open request across computers. Works without push.

  • Open requests are cards with a vermilion edge: the full command or question, where it runs, and a countdown against its real limit (“4:12 of 5:00”).
  • The question shows the options it offers, so the answer can be decided before opening it.
  • Resolved requests say who answered: you, the terminal, or the timeout. Nothing scores how fast you were.

S10

Question

The tool's own options, exactly as offered.

  • Single choice rows with descriptions, plus Write your own (one line). Multi-select questions use check boxes instead of radios.
  • 60 s because this phone has Control (15 s without). The dial drains calmly in ink, never red.
  • Send answer: a receipt with the answer, the route (hub) and the delivery time.
