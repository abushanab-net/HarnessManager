Android adaptations

One Flutter codebase and one Calibre theme: the screen bodies, type, colours and dials are the same widgets. The scaffold around them is Material 3: edge-to-edge system bars with the gesture handle, a large, medium or small top app bar with a left-aligned title and a back arrow, the NavigationBar with its active-indicator pill, an extended FAB for Needs you, stadium-shaped buttons, state layers with ripples, Material modal bottom sheets, predictive back and BiometricPrompt. Each row: iOS light, then Android light and dark.

Android · S7

Home

Large top app bar (date and title left, search and overflow right), the board unchanged, the NavigationBar, and Needs you as an extended FAB.

  • The FAB is the iOS pill's job in Android's idiom: the count and the soonest countdown, on every tab, above the navigation bar. It never pulses harder than the dot inside it.
  • NavigationBar: 80 dp on a surface-container tint, a 64 × 32 pill behind the selected icon (filled), labels always shown, the gesture handle drawn over it edge to edge.
  • State layer shown on the first request row: pressed is a 10% ink overlay with the ripple spreading from the touch point (Calibre ink, not Material's default tint). Hover 8%, focus 10%, drag 16%.
  • 412 × 915 dp (Pixel class). Haptics: a light tick when a card settles and on Approve.
Android · S9

Permission request

Medium top app bar: the arrow and overflow on top, the headline below, collapsing to a small bar on scroll.

  • FilledButton (Approve once) and OutlinedButton (Deny) in the stadium shape, 56 dp; the timer row is the same Calibre widget.
  • BiometricPrompt (fingerprint or face, whatever the phone has) replaces Face ID; the receipt is identical.
Android · S9 · gesture

Predictive back

Mid-swipe from the left edge: the request shrinks and follows the thumb, Home shows behind under a scrim, the edge arrow appears.

  • Letting go below the threshold springs the page back, so leaving an open request is never an accident. Flutter: PredictiveBackPageTransitionsBuilder, enableOnBackInvokedCallback on.
Android · S13

Stop, as a Material sheet

Modal bottom sheet: a 32 × 4 drag handle, 28 dp top corners, a 32% scrim that dismisses, left-aligned headline and text, a stadium FilledButton and a text button.

  • Back gesture, a drag down or a tap on the scrim cancels; Stop is never the default focus. The session sits under a small top app bar.

S24 · The desktop's side

In Harness Manager's own desktop style (its teal Chrono-Runway tokens), not Calibre. Computers gets a separate Pair a phone… button next to Invite a computer: a phone is never a full peer. The dialog shows both halves at once: the code the phone used, and the approval with the matching words and the grants. Control is off unless ticked.
