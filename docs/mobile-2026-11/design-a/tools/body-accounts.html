S14

Accounts

Room left on every account, grouped by what to do with it.

  • Groups instead of a sort menu: Use next, Low, Room to spare, No limit window. The first glance answers the question.
  • Each dial is the week's room; the other windows (5-hour, Fable) sit under it as small bars.
  • Low is amber with an icon and the word. A forecast carries ≈. Accounts with no limit window show a dash, never a zero.
  • One line says how old the readings are; a reading older than an hour gets its own age chip.
