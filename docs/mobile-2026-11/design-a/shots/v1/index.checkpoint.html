Calibre

Every number in this app is about time: how much room an account has before its reset, and how long a session will wait for you. A good watch has shown time and reserve at a glance for two centuries, with ticks, hands and subdials. Calibre borrows that geometry and typography, and none of the fake metal. Enamel and paper in daylight, a night dial after dark, ink for readings, and one vermilion hand kept for the thing that needs you.

One dial for every limit

Room left and time to reset share one shape, from the Home widget to the reset clock to the lock-screen gauge later. Learn it once.

Calm, honest time

Countdowns tick in ink and never turn red. Each one says what happens at zero. Every reading carries its age, estimates carry ≈, unknown is a dash.

An instrument, not a toy

XP is a power reserve, the streak is a date wheel, badges are engraved. Points only for room or time saved, shown in lume green so they never compete with work.

Design system

A compact strip for this checkpoint. The full system (spacing, elevation, iconography, sheets, list rows, tab bar variants) follows after review.

Ease-out cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1). Settle uses a light spring with one small overshoot. Dials fill on open, the countdown drains once a second, the receipt rises. With Reduce Motion everything lands at once and the pulse becomes a still ring.

Four screens

iPhone 390 × 844. Each screen has a live phone in light and dark (countdowns run, dials fill, Approve goes through Face ID to a receipt, Home cards can be dragged) and, under it, a static artboard of the whole scroll so nothing below the fold is hidden.

S18

Progress

The game layer in one place, private and traceable.

  • Badges are engraved medallions: a knurled rim, a bead ring, an enamel field and an embossed icon, with a lume jewel when earned. Locked ones are dashed with a lume progress arc around the rim.
  • Tap a badge for its detail sheet: the rule, when it was earned or what is left, and the real event behind it.
  • Tap the level card to preview the level-up.

Earned moments

The delight is kept for things that were earned, and each moment runs once, in about 600 ms. With Reduce Motion they land in their end state with no movement. Each frame opens in its end state; the button replays it.

Harvest

Saturday 18:58, if Dev01 is used first: its week resets with 4% unused. The reserve winds up, a lume tick lands, +40 rises and settles.

Level-up

Crossing 1,500 XP. The reserve winds to full, one sweep of lume ticks, the new name sets in. No confetti, no sound by default, one light haptic.
