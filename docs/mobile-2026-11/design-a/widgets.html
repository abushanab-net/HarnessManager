Widget gallery

Every family in at least two variations (A1, A2), light and dark, at real sizes. Home Screen widgets read a redacted, encrypted cache the app keeps: no titles or commands unless Settings › Widget detail allows them. Nothing inside a widget animates on its own: countdowns are the system's own timers (Text(timerInterval:), ProgressView(timerInterval:), Android Chronometer), and the timeline refreshes at each reset. Reaching zero means “refresh due”, never “limit back”. Approving, replying and Stop always happen inside the unlocked app; every widget opens it.

Constraints designed for

iOS · WidgetKit and ActivityKit

  • Families: systemSmall, systemMedium, systemLarge; accessoryCircular, accessoryRectangular, accessoryInline. No interactive approve: buttons in widgets may only run App Intents, and every action here needs Face ID, so they open the app.
  • No free animation: the only motion is the system's content transition when the timeline changes, and live timers drawn by the system.
  • Lock Screen accessories render in the vibrant (monochrome) style: one tint at varying opacity, so the dials use opacity, never colour, to separate track and arc. Amber and vermilion are lost there on purpose; the words carry the meaning.
  • Tinted and clear Home Screen modes (iOS 18+): every element is marked widgetAccentable or not; the dial arc is the accent.
  • Live Activity: up to 8 h active plus 4 h on the Lock Screen, 160 pt tall at most, updated by push (APNs, content-free until unlock unless the owner allows detail). Dynamic Island compact leading and trailing are tiny: one glyph, one timer.

Android · Glance and notifications

  • Glance (RemoteViews underneath): Compose-like layout, no custom drawing at runtime. The dials are rendered to bitmaps by the app at each update and cached; text stays real text for accessibility.
  • Resizable with SizeMode.Responsive: three layouts (2×1, 2×2, 4×2 or 4×4) chosen by the launcher size, not by scaling one layout.
  • Updates: WorkManager at each reset and on push, at most every 15 minutes otherwise; a countdown uses Chronometer, which the system ticks.
  • Ongoing notification: a foreground-service style notification while a session is followed; on Android 16 it uses ProgressStyle as a Live Update, which can be promoted to a status-bar chip.
  • Calibre colours are fixed in widgets (not Material You dynamic colour), so the semantic roles stay true; a “Match wallpaper” option can follow later.
