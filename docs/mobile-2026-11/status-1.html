Harness Manager · Mobile companion · Status · 26 Nov 2026

The app runs on your phone

Since the design was approved, the desktop learned to pair with phones and serve them, and the Flutter app was built and tested on your moto g15. On your network or VPN, the app pairs with your computer and answers its permission requests and questions, with an honest receipt for each. It also shows accounts, limits, sessions and computers, has the game layer, and has the Use next widget and the ongoing notification. Every security-relevant part went through independent Codex or Claude reviews until it passed, or was switched off when you chose so. This page shows what works, what is held back and why, and what I need from you next.
