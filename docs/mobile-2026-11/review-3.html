02Registration and activation

Pair with a computer (QR or a six-digit code), check the three words match on both screens, the computer grants Summaries, Transcripts and Control, then the private notification prompt. No account to sign up for.

03Home and requests

Home, Requests, Permission, Question and "back in the terminal". The Needs-you pill (count and soonest timer) sits in every tab's title bar. Permission now shows the session title on one line, then Claude's last words before asking.

Permission context after the fix: iOS · Android

04Sessions and Stop

Sessions grouped by state, the transcript with the reply bar, a session without Control, the Stop sheet and its two honest outcomes: "Interrupt delivered" with the computer's confirmation, or "Delivery unknown" (never sent twice).

05Accounts, projects, computers

Accounts sorted into Use next, Low and Room to spare. Account detail now answers on the first screen (about 1.2 screens, was 2.4): the reset clock, the decision card with the harvest window, then the three windows; forecast, 7 days and sessions are tabs below.

06You, progress and the game layer

Progress has level and next step above the fold, the streak as one row, and Badges / XP log / Recap as tabs. Small earned moments: a tick lands when a request is answered, the streak date wheel turns, the harvest power reserve winds up, one restrained level-up. Never for approvals or spend.

07Empty, asleep, offline, removed, update

First sync, computer asleep (snapshot, actions off), hub unreachable, phone offline (cached, every reading aged and marked ≈), access removed, update needed. Unknown is a dash, never a zero.

08Largest text size and the two sheets

Home and Permission at AX5 (body 53 pt): the board becomes one column of plain rows and the buttons stack. Plus the Sessions filter sheet and the Limit-low threshold sheet.

09Android adaptations

The same screen bodies in a Material 3 scaffold: top app bar, NavigationBar with the active pill, an extended button for Needs you, a Material bottom sheet for Stop, fingerprint instead of Face ID, and a predictive-back preview. One open nit: on Android Home the floating Needs-you button covers part of the Next reset card, so the content needs bottom padding.
