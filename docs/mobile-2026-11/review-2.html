01The two directions

The live prototypes run: dials fill, countdowns tick, Approve goes through Face ID to a receipt, the harvest and level-up moments play, and Home cards can be dragged (in A). Click any image to open it full size.

ACalibre

Claude Code · Opus 5.5 · account claude-main · 18 + 11 min

A precision watch, not a gadget: dials and subdials for every limit, a serif for readings (Newsreader) and a clean grotesk for text (Hanken), enamel and paper by day, a night dial after dark. One vermilion accent is kept only for what needs you, and lume green only for XP. Calm, crafted, very legible.

BSplit Time

Codex · Astra 6.2 (gpt-6.2-astra) · account codex-main · 11 + 11 min

A sports timing panel: condensed italic numerals (Barlow Condensed), a flip-board reset clock, coral for room and ice blue for requests, dark first. Badges are race bibs and timing chips, and harvesting “flips a lap split”. Energetic and game-forward, still disciplined.

What I asked each designer to fix after the first checkpoint, and what came back. Both addressed every point; I checked each on the rendered screens.

VerdictThe most crafted and trustworthy of the two: superb hierarchy and honesty, and the dial language carries from Home to the widgets. Risk: it can still read as more calm than fun. Medium-density screens are long (Account and Progress scroll about 2.4 screens).
