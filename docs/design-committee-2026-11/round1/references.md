# References, for the designers to choose from

The owner's mood in three words: **precise, live, future.** The owner gave no references of their own and asked that
the designers pick. Below is a starting list from a web search on 6 Nov 2026; choose what fits your angle, add your
own, and name the ones you used in your `angle.md`. These are inspirations, not templates: nothing here is to be copied.

## Calm, product-grade dashboards
- Linear (linear.app): progressive disclosure, whitespace over density, a single accent.
- Vercel dashboard (vercel.com): restraint; one key metric before a wall of charts.
- Raycast (raycast.com): keyboard-first, a command palette as the primary navigation.
- PostHog (posthog.com): insight cards that each answer one question.
- Grafana (grafana.com): the reference for dark-mode monitoring, dense but systematic.
- Roundups with screenshots: https://925studios.co/blog/saas-dashboard-design-examples-2026 ,
  https://www.muz.li/blog/best-dashboard-design-examples-inspirations-for-2026 ,
  https://adminlte.io/blog/25-best-dashboard-design-examples-for-2026-real-products-principles/ ,
  https://dev.to/thekitbase/saas-dashboard-design-trends-in-2026-with-examples-121m

## Live, mission-control, instrument panels
- Dribbble collection "Data Visualization" by blackflagcreative (stadium operations, submarine tactical, defense
  dashboards): https://dribbble.com/blackflagcreative/collections/7623989-Data-Visualization
- "Mission Control" design system (dark command centre, amber telemetry, monospace):
  https://open-design.ai/plugins/design-system-mission-control/
- COSMOS aerospace operations concept (glassmorphic dark, terminal log feed):
  https://contra.com/community/V3MGeTUd-introducing-cosmos-advanced-real-time-aerospace-operations

## Depth, 3D and WebGL
