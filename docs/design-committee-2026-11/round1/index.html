The committee's record

What every critic agrees a winning design must have

  • Your three answers as three readable statements above the fold at 1280, each in its own place, with exactly one primary Start.
  • Two aligned bars per account (5-hour, week), each with "% left" and its reset countdown, exhausted first, not-read dashed; measured / ≈ / — told apart without a legend.
  • Sessions shown per named computer, each card saying account + project, directly followable.
  • State kept in JS and a DOM patched by key or rebuilt with scroll, focus, open surfaces and animation phase restored, so the redraw every few seconds is invisible.

What it must not have

  • A slogan, a lecture title or an explanation line where the finding should be; tokens-per-second on the Overview; a capability matrix the app contradicts.
  • A bar, ring or timeline whose length or colour encodes something other than what the label beside it says; decorative or stale precision.
  • A diagram the lazy user has to learn before the page answers.

Parts worth combining (named by three or more critics)

  • A's answer strip and per-computer session board, and its keyed patching.
  • B's capacity ledger as the hero instrument and as the Accounts rows; B's one-screen Add account.
  • C's harvest-before-reset rule and reset-gate labels inside B's reset column; C's ring with the left/used switch in the tray.

Where the committee splits, for you to settle

  • Shell: A removes the sidebar (top bar + tabs + gear); B and C keep a trimmed sidebar. The craft and lazy-user lenses credit A's removal; the build critic prices it at about four extra days.
  • The hero: A's live board (what is running where) versus B's ledger (which account has room). Critics who rank A first say the glance wins; critics who rank B first say the ledger is the honest instrument and the cheaper build. Several suggest both: A's strip and board with B's ledger as the Room list.
  • C: no critic ranks it above third after fixes; its ideas (time to reset, harvest rule) are recommended as parts, not as the base.
