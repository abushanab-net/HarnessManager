Three lanes, so the three designs differ on purpose

A proposal. Rewrite any of them in question D4.

A · The living instrumentSpatial and animated: the state of your accounts and sessions as one scene (3D or WebGL-grade 2D) that you read at a glance; everything else is a layer that slides in. The wow is motion with meaning.
B · The calm editorialTypography-led: one finding, large; one action; everything else folded behind progressive disclosure. The wow is restraint: a page that feels like a well-set magazine spread and never scrolls on the Overview.
C · The operator's consoleDense on purpose but ordered: a strict grid, a command palette, keyboard-first, every number aligned, one accent. The wow is the feeling of a professional tool that answers before you finish the question.

How a round runs

  1. Brief. I write one brief from your answers: the jobs the Overview must do, the feature inventory it may not lose, the constraints, the lanes. Everyone gets it, plus these screenshots and the real data shapes.
  2. Design, in parallel. The three designers each produce their HTML mockups (Overview, shell, add account; light and dark). They do not see each other.
  3. Critique, in parallel and isolated. The judges, the lazy user and the critics each read the three designs and write their critique and scores. They never see the designers' reasoning, only the pages.
  4. Debate. Each designer answers the critiques once (a written rebuttal and, where cheap, a revised page). The judges then settle their disagreements in one written exchange and produce a record: what a good design here must have, what it must not, and the final scores.
  5. Your pick. One HTML page: the three designs side by side with a theme switch, the scores, the record, and a form like this one. You pick one, or name the parts you want combined.
  6. All pages. The winner's designer leads; the other two take pages in the winning direction; the same critics review every page; you review in one HTML page per round, section by section as before; then it goes into app/ui.

Cost: a round with nine seats is roughly ten to fifteen times a normal chat's tokens. Say in question E3 how many rounds you will pay for.
