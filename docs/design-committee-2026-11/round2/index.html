The committee's record

Is it one app? Visually yes: rail, top bar, page head, cards, chips, rings, the teal primary and both themes are shared by all three hands; no text under 11 px, no overflow, monospace confined to paths, commands and logs, Still truly stills, AA contrast everywhere. Operationally not yet: three ⋯ menu styles (modal vs popovers), three tour styles (B's auto-opening modal, the lead's three-paragraph card, none on A's pages), two dialog footers, "Preview" captions inside product dialogs, B's second fixture bar, and three separate data stores so pages disagree (Routing vs Overview routes, Sessions counts under a filter).

The three worst defects in the whole app, as the critics rank them

  • Account detail draws limit bars as used share while every other bar of that shape means % left; the tray popup is unreachable by keyboard.
  • B's tour modal opens on arrival at five Setup pages before the page is seen; B's observer and document-level listeners reach outside the registry into the lead's rail and top bar.
  • Cross-page data disagreements (routes, counts, installed tools, terminal preference) and notices leaking between pages ("Preview: Saved prompt." inside Add account).

Before it can be built as one app/ui codebase (build critic)

  • Remove the 21 inline on*= handler attributes (the app's CSP blocks them in WebView2) and move the 180–300 inline styles on the base pages to classes, or the VektraGrid theme cannot be applied.
  • One dialog and menu controller, one notice channel, one data store; A's and B's pages hook into the registry only.
