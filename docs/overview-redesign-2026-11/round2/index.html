What you said, and what changed

The logoThe sidebar now carries the real Harness Manager mark from docs/logo.svg. The VektraGrid themes carry the VektraGrid symbol: the 03 full-colour one on ivory, the 06 warm-gold one on deep green. Tokens per secondGone from the page. The scene opens with the finding instead. Beads still move in proportion to traffic, because that is what makes a route look alive, but no number claims it. The findingLeads every variation, in the display face of the theme: Inter for Harness, EB Garamond for VektraGrid. Multi-themeTwo brands, each light and dark, switched at the top of this page. Colours are roles, not hues: the signed-in route, the route through the router, a limit getting close, something broken. VektraGrid gives those roles deep teal, royal plum, sand gold and a muted red.
The orbitAstra's main correction: the setup is a shared graph, not a tree. Claude Max runs on two computers; Claude Code serves two accounts; the router serves two tools. So no layout can promise zero crossings by assigning each account a wedge. Each of the three maps makes a different trade: Atlas keeps every body in one place and routes crossings through corridors; Dial fans the hops upward from the computers so most routes never meet; Transit gives every account its own lane and draws a shared body once, spanning the lanes it serves. Kept from OrbitLimit arcs around each account (inner 5-hour, outer weekly, solid is used, dashed is the estimate at the reset), the readouts floating on the scene with dot-matrix trends, the porcelain light theme (now "Harness light"). The year clock stays in the page below the map; it is not shown here because this round is the map only. DroppedThe 3D tilt, the auto-rotation, the WebGL sky, the hand-placed angles. Every position now follows a rule, and labels are measured and pushed apart until none overlap.

One question for you. In the VektraGrid themes the sidebar shows the VektraGrid symbol beside the name "Harness Manager". If Harness Manager is a VektraGrid product that is right; if the theme is only a skin, say so and the Harness mark stays in every theme. There is a box for it at the bottom.

Map 1 · agreed with Astra

Route Atlas

A flat circle. The rings are the hops: your computers at the centre, then accounts, tools, the router's gate, and the providers outside. Every body has one stable place, set by what it serves, and routes travel corridors between the rings. The whole shared setup is visible at once.

The one bold thingThe complete topology in one glance: six accounts, five tools, the router and six providers, with no body drawn twice.
Its tradeShared bodies mean some corridors cross. Crossings are confined to the arcs between rings, never at a body, so a crossing is never mistaken for a connection.
MotionBeads on every live route at low strength; select a body and only its chain moves while the rest fades.

Map 2 · agreed with Astra

Horizon Dial

A half circle rising from the baseline. Your three computers dock on the line; accounts, tools and providers fan upward on their arcs, with the router as a gate only routed traffic passes. The finding's route is selected when the page opens, and it is the only thing that moves.

The one bold thingThe finding selects a route across the whole dial: open the page and you are already looking at the account that will run out, and everything it touches.
Its tradeHalf the circle means wider spacing per hop and a reading direction (up and out), at the cost of a taller panel; the docks keep the three computers from crowding one centre.
MotionOnly the selected route carries beads. Hover, focus or click selects another; Escape or a click on empty space returns to the finding's route.

Map 3 · Astra's candidate, accepted

Transit Orbit

Six racetracks, one per account, ordered so that shared bodies sit beside each other and are drawn once, spanning the lanes they serve. The top straight is the route, read left to right: computers, account, tool, router, provider. The bottom straight is that account's capacity: used, the estimate to the reset, and where it runs out. Beads circulate the whole loop.

The one bold thingEvery route reads as one sentence, and the loop closes on the account's capacity, so route and limit sit on the same orbit.
Its tradeNo crossings at all, because nothing is radial; the orbital character is in the loop, not in rings. Groq, which no lane reaches, hangs off the router as a stub.
MotionBeads circulate each lane in proportion to its traffic; a lane with an expired login is dashed and still. Hover a body and every lane it serves lights.
