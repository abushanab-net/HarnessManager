What every page shares

The findingEach page opens with one sentence of what matters now, as the Overview does, with the thing to act on in amber. Under it, the basis: counts, range, what is measured and what is estimated. Quiet bodyTables and cards say names and numbers; detail, warnings and actions appear where something needs them. Open sessions, limits near their end and paused providers speak for themselves. One instrument a pageSessions: today as a ribbon, a lane per computer. Projects: where the tokens went. Accounts: the limit rings from the Overview beside each account. Routing: the routes as a small map in the dial's language. Session: the conversation with its steps.
Icons and coloursEvery harness shows its official icon, in rows, column heads, filters and tabs. Computers keep their colour dots; the Harness Manager mark stays in every theme. Measured or ≈Tokens, requests and the router's timings are measured and plain. Costs at list prices, first-reply times read from logs and run-out forecasts carry ≈. SizesThe shared foundation: rem spacing, fixed reading sizes, headings and key numbers in clamp(), container queries at 70rem and 56rem of sheet width, the sidebar folding below 75rem of app width. No page has a pixel width of its own any more.
